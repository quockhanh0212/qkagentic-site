An operations agent for one-person businesses.

Vista handles the inbox, the calendar, the paperwork and the research that a solo founder would otherwise hire someone for. It runs on hardware its owner controls, and it asks before anything leaves the house.

Product: Vista

Vista is a personal operations agent. You talk to it in its phone app, and it works through your own accounts and files.

Inbox and calendarReads Gmail and Calendar, writes a daily brief, flags what needs a decision and keeps track of follow-ups.
DocumentsFiles, reads and summarises PDFs and Drive documents into a plain-Markdown knowledge base that you own.
ResearchSearches the web, reads primary sources and answers with links. Good for prices, procedures and technical questions.
Scheduled workRuns recurring tasks and background jobs, and reports back only when something has changed.

How it works

  • The owner is in control. The agent and its knowledge base live on the owner's machine. Vista reaches Google with read-only permissions, so it cannot send email or edit files there.
  • It asks first. Anything that messages another person, spends money or changes an account waits for the owner's approval in the app.
  • Every change is tested before release. Each change runs through automated checks and end-to-end self-tests on a separate development copy before it reaches the live agent. Blind-graded benchmarks measure answer quality.

Status
